# Lecture 6 — Prokaryotes and eukaryotes

Key differences: nucleus, organelles, size, ribosome type.

## Notes

The membrane separates the cell from its surroundings and controls what passes through. Small nonpolar molecules diffuse across the bilayer while ions need channels or pumps. Remember to compare the two models and note where the evidence differs. Worked examples from the textbook are a good way to check the definitions.
